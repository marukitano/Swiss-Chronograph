Tap Timer is a tactile countdown timer for Pebble.

On touch-capable models, choose between 0 and 180 minutes by dragging a
vertical ruler. Every minute behaves like a physical detent, so the scale can
never rest imprecisely between two marks. Release the ruler and the animated
chevrons complete the start sequence automatically.

The running screen uses custom high-contrast pixel digits and is deliberately
calm: minutes and seconds are shown by default, while hundredths of a second
can be enabled for timers below 100 minutes.

Controls remain immediately accessible while the timer runs: minimise at the
top left, pause or resume at the middle right, and add a minute or delete the
timer while paused.

Light, Dark and wrist-shake appearance modes are included. The read-line
colour, alarm vibration and optional speaker beep can be configured from the
phone.

Tap Timer is based on Instant Timer by Andrew Howe and was extensively
redesigned by Maru Kitano. Licensed under GPLv3.
